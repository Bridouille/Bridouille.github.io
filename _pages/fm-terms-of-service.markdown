### What the app does ###

Flight Pass keeps your boarding passes on your phone, shows their barcodes at the airport, and adds information about your flights, such as their status, gate, weather and aircraft.

### Your boarding pass is the airline's ###

 - The app shows the barcode from the pass you imported. Only the airline and the airport decide whether a boarding pass is valid.
 - Always keep the boarding pass your airline gave you, in its app, by email or on paper, in case the barcode can't be read or the airline's rules require it.
 - I am not responsible if you are refused boarding or miss a flight.

### Flight information may be wrong ###

 - Times, delays, gates, terminals, baggage belts, weather and aircraft details come from public services and can be late, incomplete or wrong.
 - Always check the information shown at the airport and the messages from your airline. Don't rely on the app alone to catch a flight.
 - Notifications depend on your phone's settings and battery optimisation, and may arrive late or not at all.
